Support

Something broken, something missing, or a question this page does not answer — use Settings → Feedback in the app, or email chriscurran28@googlemail.com. Include the app version and what you were doing when it went wrong; that is usually enough to find it.

Common questions

A score or fixture looks wrong

Vaze Sports does not hold its own results. Scores, fixtures and tables come from public sports data providers, and the app shows what they report. A correction there reaches the app on its next refresh.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. Signing in with Google is optional and only keeps your followed teams and saved articles in sync between your devices.

Deleting your synced data

If you signed in, the account section of Settings lets you remove a device or delete everything that has been synced. Uninstalling the app removes what is stored on the device.

Privacy

No ads and no tracking. What you follow stays on your device unless you choose to sign in and sync it. The privacy policy sets out exactly what leaves the app and why.
