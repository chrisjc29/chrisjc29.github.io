Vaze Sports privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

The short version. Vaze Sports works without an account. The teams and competitions you follow, your saved articles and your settings are stored on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google, those are synced through Google Firebase so they follow you between devices. The app has no ads and no tracking, and we do not sell your data.

Who this is about

This policy covers the Vaze Sports app on iOS and Android and the server it talks to. In it, "we" means the developer of Vaze Sports and "you" means the person using the app.

What stays on your device

  • The teams and competitions you follow
  • Articles you save, and your settings
  • Match reminders, which are scheduled on the device itself
  • A cached copy of what the app has shown you, so screens still open without a connection

All of it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the app sends to our server

To show scores, fixtures, tables and news, the app asks the Vaze Sports server for public sports data. Those requests name the competitions or teams being looked up, not who you are. Like any web service, the server sees the IP address each request comes from; it is used to limit abuse and diagnose faults, and is not used to build a profile of you. The server runs on Deno Deploy.

If you subscribe to a team's fixture calendar, your calendar app fetches it from the server in the same way.

Signing in (optional)

If you sign in with Google, the app uses Google Firebase to authenticate you and to store your followed teams, saved articles and the list of your signed-in devices under your account, so they stay in sync. You can remove a device or delete all synced data from the account section of Settings at any time. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, the server stores what you wrote, the kind of feedback, basic app and device details the app attaches (such as the app version) and the IP address it came from, so it can be read and acted on.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or cross-app tracking identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No collection of your location, contacts, photos or health data

Third-party content

Scores, news and images come from public sports data providers. Opening an article or other link takes you to that site, whose own privacy policy applies.

Children

Vaze Sports is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can ask what data is held about you, or ask for it to be deleted, using the contact address below. If you are in the UK, EU or another region with data-protection rights, we will help with any request we can.
