Nolvo

Your Volvo, on your wrist.

Nolvo is a companion app for Wear OS smartwatches. Check whether your car is locked, see fuel and battery levels, and send lock, unlock and climate commands — straight from your watch, without reaching for your phone.

Nolvo connects to your car through the official Volvo Cars APIs, with your explicit consent via your Volvo ID. It reads your car's data only when you ask it to, shows it only to you, and stores nothing off your device.
