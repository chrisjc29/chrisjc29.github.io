1. What the App does

The App lets you view your vehicle's status (such as lock state, doors, windows, fuel and battery level) and send remote commands (such as lock, unlock, honk and flash, and climate control) from a Wear OS smartwatch. It communicates with your vehicle exclusively through the official Volvo Cars APIs.
