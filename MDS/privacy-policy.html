MDS privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

The short version. MDS sends your data only to the MDS server run by the wholesaler you work for. We do not run that server and do not receive your data. The app has no ads and no tracking, and we do not sell your data.

Who this is about

This policy covers the MDS app on iOS and Android, stock and distribution software for UK medicines wholesalers. In it, "we" means the developer of MDS and "you" means the person using the app. Your employer, the wholesaler that runs your MDS server, decides how the data on that server is used and is responsible for it.

What the app stores on your device

  • The address of your company's MDS server
  • Your company's API key
  • Your staff ID
  • Your settings

All of it is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

What the app sends to your company's server

As you work, the app sends the stock, delivery, order, contract, dispatch and recall records you enter to your company's MDS server, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Those records include customer names and your staff ID on the records you create. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Demo data

If you choose Use demo data in Settings, the app runs on a sample warehouse stored on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the demo data resets when the app restarts.

Analytics and crash reports

Settings has a Share analytics switch. In the current release, the app sends no analytics or crash reports to us or to anyone else. If a future release sends any, this policy will be updated before that release, and the switch will control it.

Notifications

MDS can show notifications, such as a pick or dispatch in progress. They are created on your device. The app does not use push notifications.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or cross-app tracking identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No collection of your location, contacts or photos

Children

MDS is a workplace tool for adults. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

Your rights

Your employer holds the data on your company's MDS server, so ask them to see, correct or delete it. If you have a question about the app itself, use the contact address below. If you are in the UK, EU or another region with data-protection rights, we will help with any request we can.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date at the top.
