MDS

Stock and distribution for UK medicines wholesalers: scan stock in, pick orders first-expiry-first, and trace any batch to every customer it went to.

Common questions

How do I try it without a server?

Open Settings and tap Use demo data. Every screen then works on a sample warehouse stored on your device, with no server or account. The demo data resets when the app restarts.

How do I connect to my company's server?

Ask your MDS administrator for the server address, your company's API key and your staff ID, then enter them in Settings and tap Save and connect.

A pack won't scan, or the stock looks wrong

MDS shows what your company's MDS server holds. If a pack's code is refused at goods in, the screen says why, for example an expired or unknown pack. For stock that looks wrong, check with whoever runs your server first, then get in touch if it still doesn't add up.

Privacy

No ads and no tracking. The app sends your work only to your company's MDS server. The privacy policy sets out exactly what the app stores and sends.
