What the app shows, and what it cannot promise

AniNoti does not hold its own catalogue. Titles, airing times, artwork, synopses, cast and reviews come from AniList and other public anime databases, and the app shows them as those sources publish them. Broadcasters move episodes, sources disagree, and some times are estimates until a source confirms them. So a time in AniNoti can be wrong, and a reminder can be early, late or missing.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone and delivered by its operating system. They depend on notification permission, your quiet hours and how your phone manages battery, none of which we control. AniNoti is a convenience for following anime. Please do not rely on it for anything that matters more than catching an episode.

AniNoti is not a streaming service and does not host any episodes. Trailers play from YouTube or Dailymotion. "Where to watch" links take you to services that have their own terms, and once you leave AniNoti, their terms apply.
