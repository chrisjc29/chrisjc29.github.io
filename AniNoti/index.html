AniNoti

Follow anime, see what is airing this week or this month, and get a reminder the moment a new episode lands.

Support

Something broken, something missing, or a question this page does not answer — email support@aninoti.app. Include the app version from Settings → About and what you were doing when it went wrong; that is usually enough to find it.

Common questions

A reminder did not arrive

Three things account for almost all of these, in order of likelihood:

  • Notification permission is off. AniNoti shows a banner when this is the case. Turn it back on in your device settings, under Notifications for AniNoti.
  • Quiet hours held it. A reminder that falls inside your quiet hours is held until they end rather than dropped. Check Settings → Quiet hours.
  • Battery optimisation. Some Android manufacturers stop background work aggressively. Excluding AniNoti from battery optimisation in your device settings fixes it.

The schedule looks out of date

Airing times come from AniList, and a series that has not been updated there will not be updated in AniNoti. If the app cannot reach AniList it shows the last schedule it downloaded, with a banner saying how old it is, rather than an error.

An episode is missing, or a time is wrong

AniNoti does not hold its own catalogue — every title, time and description comes from AniList. Corrections made there flow into the app. If something is wrong in AniNoti but right on AniList, that is a bug worth emailing us about.

The ads, and how to change what they use

AniNoti is free, and the slots marked Sponsored between the rows of the Airing, Following, Search and Alerts lists are how it pays for itself. A list carries at most three, never in its first six rows, and there are no full-screen ads at all.

Nothing you do in the app is used to choose them — not what you follow, not what you search for. In the UK and the EEA you are asked for consent before the first ad is requested, and Settings → About → Ad privacy options reopens that form whenever you want to change your answer. Elsewhere, your device's own controls apply: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS, Settings → Google → Ads on Android.

Reporting a review or other content

Reviews are written by AniList users and shown in the app unedited. To report one, use the Report action on the review itself, or email support@aninoti.app with the series and reviewer name. Reports are reviewed within one business day, and anything found to breach our content rules is hidden from the app.

Privacy

No accounts and no sign-in. What you follow, and what you search for, stays on your device — it is never sent to us. AniNoti is free and carries ads, which is the one place data leaves the app: the privacy policy sets out what Google's ad system receives, what the Android build collects for crash reporting, and why the iOS build collects nothing of its own.
